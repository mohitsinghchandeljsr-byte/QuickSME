Here’s a **complete list of key features of Tally** (latest version: **TallyPrime**, successor of Tally ERP 9) — grouped by functional area for clarity:

---

## 🧾 1. **Accounting & Financial Management**

Tally is best known for its robust and easy-to-use accounting system.
**Features include:**

* **Voucher-based Accounting:** Record transactions using different voucher types (Sales, Purchase, Payment, Receipt, Contra, Journal, etc.)
* **Double-entry system:** Ensures error-free bookkeeping.
* **Multi-currency support:** Manage foreign currency transactions with automatic exchange rate adjustments.
* **Cost Centres & Profit Centres:** Track income and expenses by department, branch, or project.
* **Interest Calculation:** Automatic calculation of interest on outstanding amounts.
* **Cash & Bank Management:** Real-time cash flow tracking, cheque management, bank reconciliation.
* **Financial Statements:** Auto-generation of Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ratio Analysis.

---

## 💼 2. **Inventory & Stock Management**

Helps businesses maintain real-time control over stock.
**Features include:**

* **Inventory tracking by batch, lot, and serial number.**
* **Godown (warehouse) management** — manage multiple storage locations.
* **Reorder level & stock alerts** — automatic reminders for restocking.
* **Stock valuation methods:** FIFO, LIFO, Average cost, etc.
* **Bill of Materials (BOM)** — ideal for manufacturing units.
* **Multiple units of measure** and conversion support.

---

## 📊 3. **Reporting & Analytics**

Tally provides hundreds of pre-built reports and customization options.
**Features include:**

* **Dynamic Reports:** Balance sheet, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Stock Summary.
* **Cash Flow and Fund Flow statements.**
* **Aging analysis** for payables and receivables.
* **Comparative reports:** Period-wise and group-wise comparisons.
* **Scenario Management:** Create what-if financial scenarios without affecting real data.
* **Graphical Dashboard:** Summarized KPIs and performance insights.

---

## 👥 4. **Payroll & Human Resource Management**

Tally’s payroll module helps manage employees and salaries.
**Features include:**

* **Employee database management** (personal, professional details).
* **Salary structure creation** (earnings, deductions, benefits).
* **Automated Payroll processing.**
* **Statutory compliance:** PF, ESI, Professional Tax, Income Tax.
* **Payslip generation** and employee reports.
* **Leave and attendance management.**

---

## 💰 5. **Taxation & Compliance**

Tally simplifies GST and other statutory requirements.
**Features include:**

* **GST (Goods & Services Tax):**

  * Auto tax computation on transactions.
  * GSTR-1, GSTR-2A, GSTR-3B generation and reconciliation.
  * E-invoicing and E-way Bill generation.
* **TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)** and **TCS (Tax Collected at Source)** support.
* **VAT, CST, Excise, Service Tax** (for older data).
* **Audit & compliance tools** for verification and internal control.

---

## 🌐 6. **Multi-User, Multi-Company, and Remote Access**

* **Multiple companies:** Manage several businesses in one Tally installation.
* **Multi-user environment:** Concurrent access with role-based permissions.
* **Remote Access:** Tally.NET allows secure login from anywhere.
* **Data synchronization:** Between branches or remote offices.
* **ODBC Connectivity:** Import/export data with Excel or other databases.

---

## 🔐 7. **Security & User Management**

* **User roles & access control:** Define what each user can view or modify.
* **Password protection and audit trails.**
* **TallyVault:** Encrypts company data for enhanced security.
* **Activity log:** Tracks who did what and when.

---

## 🏭 8. **Manufacturing Features**

* **Bill of Materials (BOM).**
* **Job work & process costing.**
* **Material consumption tracking.**
* **Production & wastage reports.**

---

## 🧠 9. **Customization & Integration**

* **Tally Definition Language (TDL):** For custom modules and automation.
* **TallyPrime Developer:** Tool for extending Tally features.
* **API integration:** Possible with third-party systems (ERP, CRM, etc.).
* **Excel import/export:** Seamless data movement.

---

## 📱 10. **Usability & Interface Features**

* **Simple, menu-driven interface** (keyboard-friendly).
* **Multi-lingual support** (English + Indian languages).
* **Search & Filter** for quick access to reports.
* **Data migration** tools from older versions.
* **Auto backup & restore** options.

---

## 🖥️ 11. **TallyPrime (latest version) highlights**

* **Simplified navigation** (no complicated menu paths).
* **“Go To” search bar** to quickly find any report or feature.
* **Enhanced UI with better clarity.**
* **Browser access for reports.**
* **E-invoice & E-way bill integration.**
* **Real-time data sync between head office and branches.**
